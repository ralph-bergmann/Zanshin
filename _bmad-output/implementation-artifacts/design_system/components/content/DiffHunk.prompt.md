One hunk inside a `DiffView`: its color-words lines, unified or side by side, with one checkbox per part and gap rows for hidden lines. No header.

~~~jsx
<DiffHunk selectable parts={[null, 0]} picked={picked} onTogglePart={toggle} onExpand={open}
  lines={[{ t: "unchanged", n: 31, top: true }, " 58:58|  final res = await _http.post(_url, body: b.encode());", "~59:59|  if (res.statusCode [-!= 200-]{+== 409 && attempt < 3+}) {"]}/>
~~~

- **No header** (2026-11-15, Story 10.43, design `Calm Inspector Study.html`): no row with the range, the enclosing symbol, a parts pill, ± counts, a hunk checkbox or a grip. Hunks follow each other with a 1px `--fl-border` line; where a gap row sits, the gap row is the separator.
- **Parts** (Stories 10.38, 10.43): one hunk can hold several runs of changes — jj's diff-editor sections. Pass `parts` (each line's part, null for context); without it the changed lines are one part. A selectable hunk shows a 22px gutter before the numbers (side by side: leading the row) with **one 13px checkbox per part, on the part's first row** — also when the hunk has one part ("Pick these lines" / "Unpick these lines"). A picked part's rows carry a 2px `--accent-line` bar on their left edge. While the pointer rests on a part's checkbox every row of that part — its gap rows included — shows the same bar; picked and hovered look the same. The smallest pick is a part, never a line. Selection drives the composer: on @ it becomes `jj commit`, elsewhere `jj split -r`.
- **Unchanged gap rows** (Story 10.43): `{ t: "unchanged", n, top, bottom }` stands for n hidden unchanged lines at their place — before the first row (`top`), between hunks or inside one, after the last row (`bottom`). One quiet 26px band: `--fl-hover` with a 1px `--fl-border` line at 60 % above and below, the gutter empty, the arrow columns over the two number columns, "N unchanged lines" (11.5px sans, `--text-faint`). With `onExpand` its controls (flat, `--text-dim`, hover `--fl-on` / `--text`): n ≤ 20 one ↕ `updown` "Show N lines" opening all; more ↓ `chevron-down` "Show 20 lines below" (not on a top gap) opening its first 20, ↑ `chevron-up` "Show 20 lines above" (not on a bottom gap) opening its last 20, and "Show all". The count then shrinks; a fully opened gap's row goes and the lines join as plain context rows (both numbers, no part). Inside a `.zs-check-reveal` pane the controls show only while the pointer or focus is in the pane; the count always shows.
- **Whitespace ignored** (2026-11-15, Story 10.41, design `Whitespace Study.html`): while the diff options ignore whitespace (`jj diff -b` / `-w`), the rows are exactly what `jj diff -w` prints — no line of the exact diff mixed in. A line that changed only in whitespace is a context line with jj's marks (the added indentation on `--add-word-bg`, removed on `--rem-word-bg`), always, in the default view too; nothing is dimmed. Lines jj skips sit in a gap row at their place (`{ t: "gap", n }`), the same band as an unchanged gap: "N more lines with only whitespace changes", then Show (`onShow`); open (`open`), the row stays above its lines, "N lines with only whitespace changes" with Hide (`onHide`). N counts only the whitespace-only lines; identical lines between them (a blank line inside a re-indented block) open with them as plain context, so the numbers never jump. Parts and picks stay jj's exact sections: any picked part opens every gap without a button (`fixed`), so a pick never takes a line you did not see. Sections that one re-indented line spans (its old side in one, its new side in another) are tied into one part: one checkbox, on its first changed row, taking all of it. A part made **only** of whitespace changes is the same band with a 135° hatch every 6px in `--fl-hover` (`{ t: "fold", n }`), its checkbox in the gutter (it is the part's first row); closed it reads "⋯ N lines changed whitespace only", then Show (`onShow`). Show / Hide follow the same reveal rule as the gap controls. Side by side: the same rows, a whitespace-only line a pair (the right cell marks the spaces), gap rows full width. A hunk with no row left goes; a file with none reads "Only whitespace changed in this file."

- **Pinned numbers** (deferred-work I1): inside a `DiffView` the hunks scroll sideways as one unit, every line as wide as the widest; the gutter and the two number columns stay at the visible left edge on an opaque backing (the well plus the line's fill), and the bar with them.
- Lines are written as in `DiffView` (`"<kind><left>:<right>|<text>"`, changed words in `[-…-]` / `{+…+}`, removed runs on `--rem-word-bg`, added runs on `--add-word-bg`; the removed tint is darker than the added one); git-sigil lines still work and are numbered from `oldStart` / `newStart`. `wordMarks={false}` (or a surrounding `DiffView` with it) drops the tint; only a changed row keeps its runs, in their side's hue, and every other row and every side-by-side cell shows only its fill and ink (DiffView › Changed words switch).
- **Side by side** (Story 5.10) is rebuilt from the same lines, so each side shows whole lines: a context line is one row; in a run of changed lines the left cells are the old lines (each old number once, its equal and removed runs joined — one old line spread over several new ones shows once, whole), the right cells the new lines, paired row by row; each cell shows its number; a conflict header spans the row. The part's checkbox and bar sit in the leading gutter; gap rows span both sides with the arrows over the left cell's numbers.
