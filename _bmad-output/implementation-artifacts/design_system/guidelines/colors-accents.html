Recommendation: C1 + D. Multicolour = Zanshin exactly as designed. A named choice re-colours only what macOS gives its accent: selection, focus, switches, checkboxes, radios, sliders, menu highlight. Gold stays — the @ working copy, the one primary action (“New change”) and the brand — and so does every data colour: add / rem, status M, the hunk band, remote, warn / conflict, project status. Settings › General › Appearance gets Accent colour: Follow macOS · Zanshin (default Follow macOS). Each family is 8 fixed tokens (--accent fill · -ink · -line · -text · -soft · -soft-hi · -ring · -select) plus the live copies --accent-live-line / -ring / -text that stay in an inactive window; on paper the light hues split a bright fill from a darker line. Measured below on the panel’s own tokens: targets fill or line ≥ 3:1 on card, canvas (and lift on night) · ink on fill ≥ 4.5 · text ≥ 4.5 · ΔE OKLab of fill, line and text to every data colour (incl. the @ text), project status and text step ≥ .06 — the accepted near-misses (status dots; Graphite and moon ≈ the text ladder) are listed, not marked.
