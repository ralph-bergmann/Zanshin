Zanshin · App Icon Studies

残心 / zan·shin /

Zanshin is the heightened awareness that lingers after an action — the archer who holds form after the arrow flies. The enso is painted in a single breath, its imperfection the whole point. Three takes on that gesture (clean, bold, dry-brushed) plus Nami · Indigo on standby in slot 4.
